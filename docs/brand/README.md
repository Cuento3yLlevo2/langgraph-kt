# Telar brand files

The logo is a small pixel-art loom. Its threads are cream, a shuttle is halfway through a pass, and
the cloth it has woven shows two nodes joined by a thread: the steps of a workflow and the edge
between them. *Telar* is Spanish for loom.

The banners use *llengües*, the flame-shaped pattern of the ikat cloth of Mallorca. The pattern
belongs to the banners, never inside the logo.

## Using the logo

- The art is drawn on a pixel grid: the loom is 21 × 21 pixels and the wordmark's pixels are exactly
  twice as big. The SVG files stay sharp at any size. Scale a PNG only by whole numbers (2×, 3×),
  and turn off smoothing, so that its pixels stay square.
- Keep a free space around the logo of at least a quarter of the loom's width.
- Do not recolor, stretch, rotate or outline it. To change the colors, change them here first.
